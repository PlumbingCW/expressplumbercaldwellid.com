About this site

Caldwell Express Plumbing operates this website to explain plumbing services and to connect property owners in Caldwell, Idaho and nearby towns with a plumber by phone. By using the site you agree to these terms.

How service works

Everything starts with a call to (208) 679-8357. A dispatcher listens to the problem, explains the next step, and arranges a visit. You receive a clear price before work starts, and you decide whether to go ahead. Appointment times depend on the plumber's schedule and on conditions that day, and same-day visits are offered when they are available.

Information on the site

The articles and service pages are general information about plumbing. They are not a substitute for an inspection of your own pipes, fixtures, and appliances. Every home is different, and a plumber who sees your system can give advice that a web page cannot.

Prices, timelines, and code requirements change. Check the current rules with the local building authority and the agency named on the relevant page before you rely on them.

If there is a gas smell, a fire, flooding near electrical equipment, or any danger to people, leave the area and call 911 first. Call our line after everyone is safe.
