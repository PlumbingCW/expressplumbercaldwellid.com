What to have ready when you call

Give the dispatcher your full street address, the town and a short description of what is happening. Say whether water is running, whether a drain is backing up and whether anyone is in danger, so the call can be handled in the right order.

If you can reach the main water valve safely, close it before you call. For a gas smell, leave the building first, then call 911 and your gas utility from outside, and call us once everyone is safe.

How travel and timing are handled

Drive times are estimates from our Caldwell base and change with traffic and the plumber's current job. For that reason the dispatcher gives you an arrival window on the call, not a promise.

Rural addresses sometimes sit on private wells and septic systems, which changes the plumbing and the rules. Southwest District Health regulates septic systems in the area, so say on the call if yours is one.

Not sure we serve your address?

Call and give the street address. The dispatcher confirms coverage and books the visit.
