What a sewer line repair fixes

A sewer line repair fixes a break, collapse, root intrusion or sag in the pipe that carries waste from the house to the public main. The first step is a camera run, because a cleaning will not help a pipe that has lost its shape.

The repair may be a short dig and replace of one section, a trenchless liner that coats the inside of the pipe, or a full replacement. The plumber picks the method from what the camera shows.

Sewer backup: what to do first

If sewage is coming up in a floor drain, shower or tub, shut off every water use. Do not flush or run the washer, because there is no place for the water to go.

Keep people and pets away from standing water, ventilate if there is an odor, and call. If the backup also affects neighbors, the City of Caldwell sewer emergency line, (208) 949-1278, should be told, because the blockage could be in the public main.

Signs the sewer line itself is damaged

Repeat backups after cleaning

A cable clears the blockage, but a root mass or a broken joint lets it return.

A sewer smell in the yard

A crack outside the house can let gas or wastewater escape.

A wet or sunken patch in the lawn

Soil washing into a broken pipe can leave a dip, and leaking sewage can green the grass.

Slow drains in every fixture

The main line is partly blocked, and a camera finds out why.

Gurgling toilets and tubs

Air is pushed through a restricted line.

Rodents or insects at drains

A broken line can give pests a path into the house.

Older homes and cast iron pipe

About 29 percent of Caldwell's housing units, around 6,500 homes, were built before 1980, according to the Census Bureau's 2020 to 2024 estimates. Drain lines in homes of that era are often cast iron or clay, which crack, flake and collect roots over decades.

Cast iron pipe rusts from the inside out. A camera shows flaking scale, a patch repair, a spot dig, a liner or a short run of PVC can fix it, and the plumber explains which one suits the pipe. Clay lines fail at the joints, where roots enter, and a liner or spot repair seals them.

Septic systems outside the city sewer

Not every property around Caldwell is on the city sewer, and a house on a septic tank has a different set of failure points. Slow drains in every fixture, gurgling and a soggy patch over the drainfield can point to the tank or leach field instead of the house line.

A plumber can check the house line and the tank outlet, but tank and drainfield repairs are permitted by Southwest District Health, which can be reached at (208) 455-5400. Say whether the property is on septic when you call so the right visit is booked.

What decides the price of a sewer repair

Sewer work depends on depth, length and how the pipe has failed. You get the price before the work starts.

Frozen ground, roots and groundwater

Freezing weather and thaw cycles move soil around buried pipe, and NOAA normals for the nearby Nampa station show about 132 days a year at or below 32 degrees F. Roots follow water into any pipe joint that has loosened.

A camera inspection after a repeated backup, before a home purchase or before major landscaping work shows how the line is doing. The sewer camera inspection page explains what the plumber looks for.
