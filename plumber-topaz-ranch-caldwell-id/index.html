Where Topaz Ranch is

A listing guide places Topaz Ranch off Marble Front Road, west of KCID Road. Virginia Park is to the east and Voyage Crossing to the west. The guide describes a country setting with open fields, names CBH Homes as the builder and says Phase One has 16 lots.

Homes listed for sale were built between 2023 and 2026, most of them in 2026. The schools listed are East Canyon Elementary, Sage Valley Middle and Vallivue High.

Homes that are new or still being finished

A home that has just been finished has its own plumbing concerns. A final plumbing inspection should be complete before you move in, and a punch list, the builder's list of items to finish or fix, should be written down.

Run every faucet, flush every toilet and open the water heater's drain valve briefly during your walk-through. Report drips, slow drains and loose fixtures to the builder in writing, and see water heater repair if the heater itself looks wrong. Small issues found in the first months are the ones a warranty is most likely to cover.

Confirm your utilities, do not assume

The City of Caldwell lists its own water department and wastewater treatment plant. Because this page cannot confirm that every Topaz Ranch lot is inside the city, check your utility bill to see who serves you.

If a property is ever on a private well or a septic system, the rules change. Southwest District Health regulates septic systems in the area, and a water line repair or a drain job looks different on a well-and-septic lot.

Soft water and the first year

Many new owners ask about a water softener in the first year. A water softener is a sensible purchase only if your water is hard, and the city has not published a figure this page could confirm.

Test the tap, then ask your builder whether a softener loop was roughed in. That decides how much plumbing installation work a softener needs. Idaho requires a permit when plumbing work is performed, according to the Idaho DOPL Plumbing Bureau FAQ. Ask whoever does the work who pulls the permit and when the inspection is booked.

If your home uses gas

The city data hub names Intermountain Gas as the natural gas provider. This page does not know which Topaz Ranch homes use gas, so check your own appliances. If yours do, a new home is the time to find the gas shutoff and the water heater's vent.

If you ever smell gas, leave the building first, then call 911 and your gas utility from outside. Do not use switches or a phone inside. A plumber can help after the utility says it is safe.
