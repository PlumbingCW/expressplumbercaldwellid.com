What toilet repair covers

Most toilet trouble starts in the tank, where a worn flapper, a loose fill valve or a bad flush handle wastes water or keeps the bowl from emptying. The plumber replaces those parts and sets the water level, which fixes most running toilets in one visit.

Trouble at the base is different. A rocking toilet, a leak around the base or a cracked bowl means the wax ring or the flange needs attention, and a toilet that clogs again and again can point to the drain behind it, which is a job for drain cleaning.

Toilet overflowing: what to do right now

Take the lid off the tank and push the rubber flapper to the bottom of the tank, or turn the oval handle behind the toilet clockwise to shut off the supply. Do not flush again.

Lay towels around the base and keep children and pets out of the bathroom, because overflow water from a toilet is unsanitary. If other fixtures are backing up too, stop all water use and call.

Common toilet problems and what they mean

Water runs after the flush ends

The flapper does not seal, or the fill valve is stuck open.

The tank keeps refilling by itself

A slow leak from the tank to the bowl is draining it.

A weak flush

Clogged rim holes, a low water level or a partial clog slow the swirl.

Water around the base

The wax ring has failed, or a supply connection is dripping.

The toilet rocks

Loose bolts or a damaged flange let the bowl move.

Gurgling in other drains

A clog beyond the toilet is pushing air through other traps.

Replacing an older toilet

About 29 percent of Caldwell's homes were built before 1980, according to Census Bureau estimates for 2020 to 2024, and many of those toilets are second or third replacements. Older toilets can use far more water per flush than current models.

A new toilet is a straightforward swap when the flange is sound. The plumber measures the rough-in distance, installs a new wax ring and supply line and checks the seal for leaks.

What decides the price of a toilet repair

Small tank repairs are quick. A replacement is priced by the toilet and the flange work. The quote is given before work starts.

Water use, leaks and Caldwell utility billing

The City of Caldwell's billing policy says it does not adjust a bill for excess water caused by a leak on the owner's side, so a silent toilet leak can show up in full on the next statement. The food coloring test in the FAQ below takes fifteen minutes and costs nothing.
