Where water travels in a two-story house

In a two-story home, the water heater is often downstairs and the bathrooms upstairs. A leaking supply line, a failed toilet connection or a loose tub drain upstairs can run down inside a wall before anyone sees it.

Watch for a brown ring on a ceiling, a damp wall, or a water meter that moves when nothing is on. Leak detection finds the source before walls are opened, and a toilet repair often fixes the most common upstairs culprit.

Newer homes still need basic maintenance

Homes built in the last few years are less likely to have old-pipe problems, but a water heater tank, a pressure regulator or a garbage disposal can still fail early. Flushing a tank and checking the shutoff valves once a year is cheap insurance in any house.

If a water heater is the problem, water heater repair explains how a diagnosis works. If a drain is slow, drain cleaning covers the usual causes.
