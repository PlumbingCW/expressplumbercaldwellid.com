What a water heater installation includes

A water heater installation sets a new unit where none existed, or moves an existing heater to a better spot. It covers the supply and return lines, shutoff valves, relief valve and discharge pipe, venting or electrical hookup, and the expansion tank where the system calls for one.

This page is for planned work such as a remodel, a fuel switch, a new garage heater or a home addition. If a tank is flooding the floor right now, go to emergency water heater replacement instead.

Sizing the heater for your household

A 40 gallon tank suits a household of two or three, while a 50 gallon unit is the usual choice for four people or a home with a soaking tub. Tankless sizing depends on flow in gallons per minute and on how cold the incoming water runs in winter.

The plumber asks how many bathrooms you have, how many people shower at the same time, and whether a dishwasher and washer run in the evening. Those answers set the size faster than square footage does.

Where the heater goes matters in Idaho winters

Many Caldwell homes keep the heater in an attached garage. NOAA's 1991 to 2020 normals for the Nampa station show an average January low near 23 degrees F and about 132 days a year at or below freezing. A garage heater needs insulated pipes, a drain pan with a drain line and a clear path for the vent.

A heater moved inside the thermal envelope costs a little more to plumb but reduces standby loss and removes the freeze risk. The plumber walks the options with you before the quote.

Permits, inspections and licensing in Caldwell

Idaho's Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses says a permit is required when plumbing work is performed, and it adds that a local building permit does not replace the state plumbing permit. The state plumbing code is the 2015 Uniform Plumbing Code with Idaho amendments. Ask the plumber who pulls the permit and when the inspection is scheduled.

The City of Caldwell Building Safety Division handles building permits and takes same-day inspection requests made by 7:00 a.m. You can check any plumber's license on the state's online license search, which lists apprentice, journeyman and contractor categories.

A tank that fails before the install date is handled as water heater replacement, and installs are also scheduled in Middleton.

Gas safety belongs in every install

A gas heater adds a gas line, a shutoff, a sediment trap and a vent. If you ever smell gas during or after an install, leave the building, call 911 and your gas utility, and stay out until it is cleared.

The plumber leak-tests every gas connection before the unit is fired, and shows you where the manual shutoff is. The same discipline applies to carbon monoxide: a working detector near the heater is cheap insurance.

After the install: the care that makes a heater last

Flush a tank heater once a year to clear sediment, and check the anode rod every two to three years. Test the relief valve every year by lifting the lever briefly with a bucket under the discharge pipe.

If a leak ever develops, close the cold valve, cut the power or gas, and use the 24/7 line. The repair page lists what the plumber checks first.
