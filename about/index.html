What we do

Caldwell Express Plumbing is a dispatch service based at 2805 Blaine St in Caldwell, Idaho. You call (208) 679-8357, a dispatcher listens to the problem and a licensed, insured plumber is booked to visit your address.

This site is call-only on purpose. A phone call lets the dispatcher hear how serious the problem is, tell you what to shut off and book the right person, which a web form cannot do at two in the morning.

How a call works

The dispatcher asks what is happening, where the water is and whether anyone is in danger. You get a clear price before work starts, and same-day appointments are offered when they are available.

Prices depend on the job, so the dispatcher and the plumber explain what affects the cost instead of quoting a number before they know the problem.

How to check any plumber

Idaho licenses plumbers through the Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses as apprentices, journeymen and contractors. You can look up any license on the state's online license search.

Ask the plumber for a license number when they arrive, and ask who pulls the permit on any larger job. Idaho states that a permit is required whenever plumbing work is performed.

What this site does and does not claim

The service and article pages are general information, written around Caldwell's climate, housing and utilities, with sources named where figures come from. They do not replace a plumber looking at your pipes.

We do not publish customer reviews, star ratings or award badges on this site. Every claim here is something the phone line and the plumbers can back up when you call.

Which problems we handle

The dispatcher books plumbers for water heaters, drains, sewer lines, leaks, burst pipes and fixtures in homes, and for restaurants, shops and offices. The full list is on the services page, and the most common emergency calls are clogged drains, water heater failures and burst pipes.

Planned work is welcome too, such as repiping, sewer camera inspections before a home purchase and water softener installation. Planned jobs are scheduled at a time that suits you and quoted before work starts.

Where our local facts come from

The pages on this site are written around Caldwell, not around a template. Housing figures come from the Census Bureau's American Community Survey for 2020 to 2024, and climate figures come from NOAA 1991 to 2020 normals for the Nampa station, about 7 miles east.

Utility, sewer, permit and licensing details come from the City of Caldwell, the Caldwell Municipal Irrigation District and the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses. Where a number is not published, such as water hardness in some city reports, the pages say so and tell you how to test your own tap.

How the 24/7 line works

The line is staffed at all hours, including nights, weekends and holidays. A call at two in the morning reaches a dispatcher, not a voicemail box, and the dispatcher books the earliest visit available.

Same-day appointments are offered when they are available, which depends on the plumber's schedule and the day's conditions. If a visit cannot happen right away, the dispatcher tells you what to shut off and what to watch for in the meantime.

What you can count on

You hear a clear price before work starts, and you decide whether to go ahead. The plumbers who visit are licensed and insured, and same-day appointments are offered when they are available.

If a problem needs a different kind of specialist, such as a gas utility for a leak in the street line or the city for a public sewer main, the dispatcher says so instead of sending a plumber to a job that is not theirs.

Five things to know before an emergency

Find your main water shutoff valve and turn it once a year so it does not seize. Know where your sewer cleanout is, because it is the access point when a main line backs up.

Check the age of your water heater, which is printed on the label, since a tank past ten or twelve years is the one most likely to fail. Learn where the breaker and gas shutoff for the heater are. Save the number, (208) 679-8357, in your phone so it is there at two in the morning.

These steps cost nothing, and they shorten any emergency visit, because the plumber can start fixing instead of searching.
