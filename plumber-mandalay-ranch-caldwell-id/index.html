A community built by several builders

A listing guide places Mandalay Ranch at the southwest corner of Middleton Road and Ward Road and counts 240 homesites. It names five builders: Stetson Homes, Sunrise Homes, Ted Mason Signature Homes, Todd Campbell Custom Homes and Lennar Homes. A real estate page also says the community has both single-family homes and townhouses.

The same guide gives lot sizes from 0.16 to 0.33 acre and lists a community park, a neighborhood pool and a trail beside Mason Creek. Woodgate is described as the neighbor to the southwest and Virginia Park to the west. The homes shown for sale were built between 2022 and 2025.

What several builders mean for repairs

Five builders can mean five sets of fixtures, valves and water heater models in one subdivision. Two neighbors with the same street name may have different parts under the sink. That is general plumbing sense, not a claim about any particular house.

Before a repair, find out which builder built your home and whether a builder warranty is still open. Read the terms, because who does a repair can affect coverage. If the problem falls outside the warranty, a plumber can usually replace a faucet, a toilet part or a supply line with the model number in hand.

Water heaters in newer homes

A newer house does not mean a water heater that never fails. A tank unit loses efficiency and eventually leaks, and a relief valve or thermostat can fail early. See water heater repair for what a diagnosis covers and water heater replacement when the tank itself is done.

If you find water around the base of a tank, shut off the water supply to it and the power or gas, then call. A slow drip that stops and starts is still worth a look.

Irrigation is a separate system

Listing filters for Mandalay Ranch mention pressurized irrigation sprinkler systems. The Caldwell Municipal Irrigation District runs pressurized irrigation for specific Caldwell communities, and its page says communities not on its list are served by other districts such as Pioneer, Caldwell Lateral or Wilder.

The page does not say which district covers Mandalay Ranch, so check your annual irrigation bill. The district says it does not blow out private lines, so a homeowner needs a sprinkler company for that. Irrigation lines are separate from the pipes inside the house.

City services around Mandalay Ranch

The City of Caldwell data hub names the Caldwell City Water Department and the Caldwell City Wastewater Treatment Plant, with Intermountain Gas and Idaho Power for fuel and power. The utility bill for your own address shows who serves it.

Repairs inside a new house sometimes need a permit as well. Idaho requires a permit when plumbing work is performed, according to the Idaho DOPL Plumbing Bureau FAQ. Ask whoever does the work who pulls the permit and when the inspection is booked.
